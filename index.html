DYRHALD

An underwater online game. Humans and fishmen share one ocean, push ever deeper, and slowly learn the abyss holds something older than either of them.

The story so far

A colony fell. You lived.

The surface died generations ago. People live below the waves now, in sealed domes and reef cities. Dyrhald was one of the few places where humans and fishmen lived side by side, until a blight rose from the deep and swallowed it.

You are one of its survivors, with no memory of the last night. Dead water is creeping upward, the reefs are bleaching, and every species blames another. The only way to the truth is down.

What you do

Dive, fight, carve, craft, go deeper

Each dive is a loop: head out, fight, gather what the sea gives you, come home, upgrade. The long goal is the next depth, where the water gets darker, colder and stranger.

Swim the whole ocean

One open sea you can swim and walk through, from sunlit sand to kelp forests and glowing caves. Diving is how you move, fight and escape.

Guns and blades

Harpoon guns, nail guns, scatter nets, gill shooters, spears, tridents and hammers. Swing, block, and land hits you can read.

Carve what you kill

No loot shower. Whatever you hunt, you carve by hand for scale, fang, meat and chitin, and better materials make better gear.

Gather and craft

Glowcoral, kelp, copper and iron ore. Every item has a quality and every material a rarity, from coral tools up through copper, bronze, iron and steel.

Your Den

A home base under the sea, with a forge, workbench, storage and a trophy wall. Sell from home, and set your hearth to wake there if you're knocked out.

A living sea

Named rare creatures in their lairs, fish migrations, predator frenzies, kelp blooms, and cultist raids on the town of Saltreach. Defend it, or don't.

Two species, one ocean

Tech or adaptation

Human divers survive on what they build: rebreathers, lamps, wetsuits and dive kit that grow with them. Their power lives in their gear.

Fishmen change their own bodies. Low-light eyes, deeper gills, pressure bladders: permanent adaptations with a cost.

Both species play together in the same world. The species changes how you see the story, not who you can group with.

Release 13.5

13 November 2026

Map

  • Meridian 13: the canyon city, with cobbled avenues, walls, a free town workbench and a town brewery beside the town kitchen.
  • Lastout, a survivors' camp on the Rift's west bench above the Dyrhald Gorge, with two new characters to meet.
  • Two new Ancient seal arches on the Twilight Shelf: Pressure Station Nine and Edgewatch.
  • Fewer named areas around Kelpwatch: quests now point you at the Blight Edge, the kelp north of camp and the wrecks south of it.
  • Fixed: some Glowcoral on the Twilight Shelf were invisible and couldn't be harvested.
  • Safe-zone rings now show on the minimap and world map only once you have charted their area.
  • Fixed a flat yellow slab under the coral houses.
  • Fixed: the Ancient seal gates' floating ring stones now look like real stone.

PvP

  • Dyrhald Below: queue as a party of 1 to 3 from any seal gate or crater drop hole, from level 18. Matches of up to 30 divers last 30 minutes, with blight rising near the end.

World

  • Seal gates: the Den's portals are now lilac Ancient seal arches. Press E at one to pick where to go: the Den, or any portal you have visited.
  • The Kelpwatch gate brightens the first time you come near it carrying Half a Locket.
  • Live testing now runs in set hours: Friday 4:00 PM to Sunday 10:00 PM Central. Outside them the start screen shows when the next session opens, and warnings come at 30, 10 and 1 minute before it closes.
  • Features open by level: founding a guild opens at 15 (anyone can join one at any level), weapon enhancement slots at 10, the auction house at 5, Dyrhald Below at 18; locked screens show their level.
  • Swimming uses a little less air: about 5 seconds from full instead of 4.
  • Fixed: "Something heard you!" now shows only when a creature really comes for you, not whenever your gathering noise meter fills.
  • Fixed: a gathering node's weak spot now moves on every hit, including Glowcoral, Branch Coral, Ember Coral and corpses, which kept one spot.
  • Fixed: a creature's name and health bar could stay stuck on screen after you left, even in your Den.
  • Fixed: the Reef Turtle's front flippers no longer jerk back and forth.
  • Fixed: the guild den no longer floats in the distance when you're in your own Den.
  • Hold E to keep swinging at a gathering node. A node's weak spot now shows only on parts still standing, and its highlight follows the broken shape, so no ghost of the node is left behind.
  • Fixed: standing in a current, your feet now ride with your body and you brace into the current, instead of being left behind.
  • Fixed: every body now runs with the human run, so a fishman's trailing foot no longer flicks out sideways each stride, and strafes use the human side steps.
  • Fixed: on a new computer your character list showed every slot empty until the server answered. It now shows your real characters.
  • Fixed: sign-in and character claims always answer now, instead of waiting forever when the data service is slow.
  • Fixed: blight and the other status meters no longer stick on the HUD after a knock-out or a hop into your Den.
  • Other players' swings now show their attack animations.
  • Working townsfolk stop and talk to you when you start a conversation, a quest or their shop.
  • Jump and hold Space to rise straight up.
  • Map pings are now marked in the world: a soft beam of light in the pinger's color, with a ring on the ground.
  • Fixed: the opening scene's live version now always ends, even when the film can't play.
  • Players far from the servers (high ping) are no longer bitten by creatures after dashing out of reach: bites that land out of reach on your own screen now miss, and hits are judged where the creature was on the attacker's screen.
  • Early elites and champions have less health at low levels (about a quarter less at level 1, easing to no change by level 10); normal creatures are unchanged.
  • Ambush lurkers now keep well away from every town's safe zone, and stay out of boss arenas (except the Vault Warden adds in Warden Prime's fight).

Quests

  • Quest lines and objectives reworded to read naturally, and the voiced lines were voiced again with natural pauses.
  • Contract suggestions start at level 3, and a suggestion that was waiting when you turned them off no longer pops up afterward.
  • First steps are now a quest, A Bench of Your Own, in the quest log and tracker.
  • Fixed: a high-level character in the Den could still see the opening's first-step objective.
  • Someone a quest needs you to talk to now has a gold ? over their head, on the compass, minimap and world map.
  • Fixed: the Bell Ledge edge lesson now counts anywhere on the rim between the two lanterns.
  • Fixed: a fishman's bench lesson now makes your own gun's bundle.

Items

  • Faction ranks: sell to a faction's own merchant to build standing. Deepworks sells gun fittings, Charge Cells and kit by rank, and at Exalted a new Drill-Lance (level 28 spear). The Called sell their charms and rings.
  • Fixed: selling at a trader sells only the item you picked; Sell 1 and Sell all no longer take the same item in other rarities or qualities too.
  • Fixed: quick deposit puts medicine, flares, ammo and other kit onto a pile the chest already holds, and its message shows in the storage window.
  • Fixed: Lamp Cells and the Quick Recharge perk now show an icon.
  • 361 item, armor, kit, food, drink, tool and bag descriptions rewritten in plain sentences.
  • Coral Dart Bundles can now only be learned and made by fishmen.

Crafting

  • Crafting lists show only recipes you have learned in the Knowledge tree (set pieces appear from their pattern). Patterns say Learn.
  • Away from a station, the bags' Crafting tab lists what you can make by hand first, then a Requires Station divider; station recipes say Needs a station.
  • The recipe panel shows materials as big squares under the words, with Craft and Track below.
  • New: a gold icon over a Den station when finished crafts are waiting to be taken.

UI

  • New visual UI update across all screens: inventory, crafting, journal, skills, hover cards, start screens and buttons.
  • The minimap no longer hitches when it first appears, and quest search areas stay inside its circle.
  • Fixed: the Den door works right after you arrive in Meridian.
  • Character creation: each species card shows its own starting weapons, with a plain-sentence line for what it starts with and what its skill trees are good at.
  • What's New waits until the opening and a new diver's lessons are done.
  • Quests is now the first journal tab and the journal opens on it. Each side quest and contract in its list has a one-click Track button.
  • Workbenches: Repair & Upgrade is now a tab on the workbench window. After your first build the station window turns to Use, the Upgrade page fills the window, and your first craft lights the queue with a one-time hint.
  • The Den storage chest opens in the same window as your stations, with Use and Upgrade tabs and its level shown as Level N of 10. Upgrading anything now plays a sound and an effect and shows a short line saying what the new level gives.
  • Item hover cards no longer list Used in. Right-click an item and choose More info to see it in full with every recipe it goes into.
  • The Knowledge tree shows at a glance what you can learn now, what needs more points, and what is out of reach.
  • Fixed: the storage chest's level now reads the same everywhere.
  • Fixed: Create Account and Login always answer: a click with something missing now says what, and the password rules show under the box, each lighting up as you meet it.
  • Updates download faster and are smaller: the launcher fetches more files at once and checks each as it arrives, and about 1,000 files (2 GB) of build backups that players never needed are no longer part of the download. Installs that already have them clear them out on the next update.
  • What's New is now an Updates tab in the journal, instead of opening over the game as you land.
  • The pale chunk on health bars drains faster.

Visuals

  • Fixed: no more white-then-black patches of light near the Twilight Shelf's lamp caches and glowing fish.
  • Fixed: characters sometimes drew plain white with no textures.
  • Fixed: the opening film no longer shows a black screen with crackling sound. It plays properly now, and if a PC can't show it, the opening scene plays live in the game instead.
  • Fixed: knees now bend out over the toes, not in.
  • Walking on the seabed feels floaty again: longer, slower strides with the body and arms drifting through each step (since 13.3 it looked quick and mechanical).
  • Guns no longer flip upside down or twist in your hand while swimming (the harpoon rifle and pressure cannon were the worst); other players see them held right too.
  • Fixed the sun's path: it now rises in the east and sets in the west, so shadows fall the right way.
  • Your knees no longer kink sideways for a moment when you stop walking.
  • Fish camp hut roofs no longer show light and dark streaks across the thatch.
  • The shallows (Kelpwatch, Tidewatch and the open shelf) are brighter by day; night and the deeper zones are unchanged.
  • Fixed harsh light and dark lines on the sunken trawler, barge and wreck section.

Audio

  • Spoken lines now pause at questions, exclamations and trailing dots instead of reading straight through.
  • Opening a quest or talk window fades out every voice still playing, so lines no longer talk over each other.
  • Fixed: journal sounds are quieter, no longer crackle, and play one at a time.
  • Every landed hit plays a short confirm sound by what you hit (flesh, shell, jelly, armor), with a bright tick on a weak point and a deep thud on a kill.
  • Music now starts at a lower default volume (0.65, was 0.8). A setting you never touched moves down once; a slider you moved keeps its value.

Known issues

  • A brief one-time stutter the first time new ground shadows appear; fix coming.
