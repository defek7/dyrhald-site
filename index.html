The story so far

A colony fell. You lived.

The surface died generations ago. People live below the waves now, in sealed domes and reef cities. Dyrhald was one of the few places where humans and fishmen lived side by side, until a blight rose from the deep and swallowed it.

You are one of its survivors, with no memory of the last night. Dead water is creeping upward, the reefs are bleaching, and every species blames another. The only way to the truth is down.

What you do

Dive, fight, carve, craft, go deeper

Each dive is a loop: head out, fight, gather what the sea gives you, come home, upgrade. The long goal is the next depth, where the water gets darker, colder and stranger.

Swim the whole ocean

One open sea you can swim and walk through, from sunlit sand to kelp forests and glowing caves. Diving is how you move, fight and escape.

Guns and blades

Harpoon guns, nail guns, scatter nets, gill shooters, spears, tridents and hammers. Swing, block, and land hits you can read.

Carve what you kill

No loot shower. Whatever you hunt, you carve by hand for scale, fang, meat and chitin, and better materials make better gear.

Gather and craft

Glowcoral, kelp, copper and iron ore. Every item has a quality and every material a rarity, from coral tools up through copper, bronze, iron and steel.

Your Den

A home base under the sea, with a forge, workbench, storage and a trophy wall. Sell from home, and set your hearth to wake there if you're knocked out.

A living sea

Named rare creatures in their lairs, fish migrations, predator frenzies, kelp blooms, and cultist raids on the town of Saltreach. Defend it, or don't.

Two species, one ocean

Tech or adaptation

Human divers survive on what they build: rebreathers, lamps, wetsuits and dive kit that grow with them. Their power lives in their gear.

Fishmen change their own bodies. Low-light eyes, deeper gills, pressure bladders: permanent adaptations with a cost.

Both species play together in the same world. The species changes how you see the story, not who you can group with.

Latest news

Release 12.3

7 November 2026

Changed

  • The sea has a new, more vivid look: sun shafts that tilt with the sun and stop at rock, light that fades with depth (Meridian's light reaches 130 m and is dark by 180 m), a brighter Shallows, better-lit characters and calmer caustics.
  • You choose your first skill at the end of the tutorial; there are no automatic starter skills.
  • Slopes steeper than 50 degrees can't be stood on: you slide off.
  • The Raid on Saltreach event is removed.
  • New menu sounds.
  • New visual UI update across all screens: inventory, crafting, journal, skills, hover cards and start screens.
  • Air upgrades are renamed as air kits (Air Tanks Mk II, Air Rebreather Mk III, Air Gill Collar Mk IV, and so on).
  • Music plays while the game loads and on the start screens.
  • The seafloor sand is smoother, with ripples only on open flat sand; brine lakes have soft shores; the Twilight Shelf's sheer walls are reshaped and repainted, and rocks, ruins and camp props there rest on the ground.
  • Meridian's lamps are dimmer, the north end has fewer rocks, and its canyon rock is redone.
  • Camp tables and workbenches, and the ones in your Den, are at waist height. The Den's time of day (Cycle, Morning, Noon, Evening, Night) is set at your Bedroll's window and only changes your own Den.
  • Tech Oyelaran's Pressure Seals offer names the Seeps' Brine Crawlers.

Fixed

  • Servers send updates 20 times a second again (they were running at about 14), so creatures and divers move smoother.
  • Loading no longer freezes for seconds or shows "Not responding", and loading music is no longer cut off.
  • Knees no longer bend the wrong way when you jump.
  • Floating rocks, coral and buildings across the shelf and the Shallows are set down on the ground.
  • Fewer camera clips through large decorations.
  • Townsfolk no longer stand in a T-pose.
  • No more bridges or ladders hanging in open water in Meridian.
  • Stronger server checks against forged actions, chat spam and action floods.
