Privacy and terms

Your data, plainly

What Dyrhald keeps about you, why, for how long, and how to have it deleted. Then the short rules of play.

Last updated 29 October 2026.

The short version

  • We keep what the game needs to work: your account, your character, what happens in game (for stats and leaderboards), chat, and the bug reports you choose to send.
  • No ads. We never sell your data. No payments. We don't record your screen or your play sessions.
  • This website has no cookies and no trackers, and it loads nothing from other companies.
  • You can ask us to delete everything, and we will.
  • You must be 13 or older to play.

Who we are

Dyrhald is made by Dyrhald Studio LLC, in the United States. Questions, or a request to see or delete your data: support@dyrhald.com, or message a moderator on our Discord.

What we collect, and why

Your account

You sign in with a username and password. Sign-in is run by our game engine provider; we never see your password. We get a player ID from them, which links everything below to you. If you choose an email address as your username, they and we can see it. Your own PC remembers your username and display name, so you don't have to type them each time.

Your character

Your character's name, look, species, level, skills, gear, bags, Den and progress are saved in our engine provider's cloud storage, so your character is the same wherever you log in. We also keep backup copies on our server.

What happens in game

Our game servers record events like logging in and out, entering an area, level-ups, deaths, quests, crafting, gathering, kills and achievements, with your player ID and character name. We use them for your stats, the leaderboards, and to balance the game. This stays on our own server; we don't use outside analytics services.

Chat

Region and party chat messages are kept on our server: the last 1,000 or so messages in each region, with older ones dropped. Guild chat is kept for 7 days. We keep chat so we can look into reports of abuse.

Bug reports (F8)

Only when you send one. A report holds your message, a screenshot if you leave that box ticked, the last two minutes of the game's log (with IP addresses, email addresses and your Windows username removed), your PC's specs (graphics card, resolution, frame rate), where you were and what you'd just done in game, and your player ID. Reports go to our server and to our private bug tracker, run by a code-hosting service. We use AI assistant tools to help read reports and fix bugs.

Fair-play checks

The game servers note unusual movement or damage that could mean cheating, with your player ID and character name, so we can look into it.

Server logs

Our game servers log your player ID and character name when you join or move between areas, to help us fix problems. Logs are overwritten after a few megabytes.

Discord account linking (not available yet)

We plan to let you link your Discord account to your Dyrhald account. Before that goes live, we'll update this page to say exactly what it stores.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing your data for marketing.
  • No payments or purchases.
  • No screen, voice or session recording.
  • No crash-reporting or analytics services from other companies.

Who else handles your data

  • Our game engine provider runs sign-in, stores your character in its cloud storage, and delivers game downloads to the launcher through its content delivery service. We've switched off the engine's own statistics and analytics.
  • Our web host serves this website, and a code-hosting service runs our private bug tracker. They may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • An AI assistant provider supplies the tools we use to help read bug reports and fix bugs.
  • Our community chat service hosts our community. We only post announcements there; the game sends no player data to it.

Our game servers run on our own machine in the United States. These providers may store data in the United States or other countries.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Write to support@dyrhald.com or message a moderator on Discord with your username. We'll delete your account, character, stats, leaderboard entries, guild messages and bug reports within 30 days. Copies in backups disappear as the backups expire. If you're in the EU or UK, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Age

Dyrhald is for players 13 and older. If we learn that a younger child has an account, we'll delete it.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we'll say so on the news page and in Discord.

Terms of play

By playing Dyrhald you agree to these:

  • You're 13 or older.
  • It's an alpha. The game is provided as it is. It can break, change, go offline or be reset, and we can't promise it will always be there.
  • Be decent. No harassment, hate, threats or sharing other people's personal details. No cheating, botting, or exploiting bugs on purpose. Report bugs instead. We can mute, kick or ban accounts that break these rules.
  • Your account is yours. Keep your password to yourself and don't share or sell your account.
  • In-game items have no real-money value and can't be sold or traded for money.
  • Dyrhald, its world, art and story belong to Dyrhald Studio LLC. You're welcome to stream, record videos and share screenshots.
  • Feedback and bug reports you send us can be used to improve the game.
