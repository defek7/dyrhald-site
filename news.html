Release notes

What changed in each alpha release, newest first. The same notes show up in the game's "What's new" window.

Release 11.6

29 October 2026

New

  • The Knowledge tree (Skills, Knowledge tab): you learn recipes with Knowledge points as you level. Your character keeps everything it could already make.
  • A Neck slot. The Tide Charm is now a necklace: wear it and press H to go home to your Den.
  • Downed and revive: when you go down, a friend can bring you back before you're knocked out.
  • Guild invites by name to anyone in the region, online or not. An invite waits 24 hours.
  • Choose your wetsuit colour when you make a character.
  • A new starting harpoon gun, the ornate harpoon rifle, and new human bodies, male and female.
  • Armour is shaped to your own body, so it fits every build and pose, and other players now see what you wear.
  • In PvP, an enemy's health shows when you hit them, and dash lunges and Rending Lunge now hurt players.
  • Meridian's people speak their new lines aloud.
  • A redesigned weapon mastery page: a card for each weapon family with your rank and progress.
  • Before your first dive, the game tells you it's an online game with chat and accounts, for ages 13 and up.

Changed

  • The achievement wetsuits are now titles: Lurker Bane, Into the Twilight and Green Fingers.
  • Fishmen and humans share one metal gun upgrade path. Fishman grafts become the matching fittings, and your gun tier and Knowledge points are kept.
  • The Skills window has tabs: Skill trees, Knowledge, Weapons and Trades, with your unspent points shown on them.
  • A red name tag now means a creature is hunting you right now.
  • The dash is shorter.
  • PvP shots count when they pass close to a player's body.
  • Kelp is cut from where you are, at your shoulder.
  • Swimming looks better: your feet stay pointed and the kick is calmer.
  • New models for the wardsteel armour, the bronze, steel and wardsteel dirks, the ingots, the floats, the brews, the meals and kelpcloth.
  • Hair is now built on your head, like armour.

Fixed

  • The Razorfin no longer lifts you up: its dash passes through you.
  • Gaps in the seafloor at the Shellback bowl, the Drop-off's lip and the starting plateau are closed, and so is the crack up the Bell Ledge pillar.
  • Meridian's ramps and walkways connect properly.
  • Harpoons stuck in things keep their own size.
  • A carving weak spot never lands on a harpoon stuck in the body.
  • A Den hop or relog no longer refills your gun or pouch.
  • Creatures no longer live inside boulders, and your game no longer keeps creatures that aren't there.
  • The Drowned Light's broken wall no longer has a see-through gap.
  • E answers in quest dialogs.

Release 11.5

27 October 2026

New

  • The map and minimap are redone: shaded by depth, with height lines, a soft shoreline and names laid out so they don't overlap.
  • Crafting at a station shows everything about the item under Craft (what it does, its numbers, how it compares with your weapons), with no hovering.
  • Contracts can be handed in to whoever posted them, as well as at the board.
  • The edge of the world has cliffs and a mountain ridge along it.

Changed

  • Station craft queues grow with the station's level: 3 at level 1, up to 12.
  • Rarity colour now means a rolled copy: plain materials and gear show as Common (a Lurker Fang is white, a Rare Lurker Fang blue).
  • The picked item's card in your bags is a short strip at the bottom, so the bags get more room.
  • Station upgrade previews read more clearly.
  • Walking on the seafloor is 5% quicker.
  • You're turned back a little sooner at the edge of the world.
  • Dens are sheltered from the weather and the sea's events.

Fixed

  • Selling at a trader near a named rare no longer takes several clicks.
  • Carving right after a fight no longer drops cuts and then hands out everything at once, and the glint waits until the find is in your bags.

Release 11.4

27 October 2026

New

  • Sort buttons for your bags, your Den chest and guild chests.
  • Station crafting has categories down the left, with how many of each it can make.
  • Weapon tooltips compare a weapon with both of yours.
  • Your bags are split into sections, and slots are shaded by rarity.
  • Icons for 78 items that had none.
  • Riptide and Pressure Wave have big new looks.

Changed

  • The melee Bronze Trident is now the Bronze Fishing Spear, and the Wardsteel Trident is the Wardsteel Pike.
  • Thin item icons (harpoons, knives, spears) no longer have a white rim, and the rifle icon is the right way up.
  • Critical-hit glints always show on top of the node.
  • NPCs stand with their palms resting at their sides.

Fixed

  • Loot chests, crates and barrels now fade into the murk like everything around them.
  • The Crafting tab no longer lags.
  • The "Choose your first skill" objective clears once you've picked a skill.
  • Right-click Use works on the Tide Charm again.

Release 11.3

26 October 2026

Changed

  • Item icons are easier to read: each item fills its icon, with brighter light and a pale rim.
  • Meridian has two new houses: a hull house on the west deck and a whale-rib house on the east deck.

Fixed

  • The bright cyan discs and streaks in the Hull Graveyard, the Rimlands and by the Wreck of the Mariah are gone.
  • The thin black line along the top of sand ridges is gone.
  • Shots, beams, lines and other effects no longer hang in the water for good.

Release 11.2

23 October 2026

Changed

  • A creature's name colour now shows how it behaves toward you: red means it hunts you, amber means it fights back, green means it flees. Its glow and badge show its tier, and named rares are gold.

Fixed

  • A Razorfin's dash no longer bounces you up and down: it skims past your side instead.
  • Creatures no longer shoot straight up steep hills.
  • A named rare you carve alone no longer comes up empty.
  • The bright cyan glow at the foot of the Wreck of the Mariah is gone.
  • Lights settle down properly as soon as the world loads.

Release 11.1

23 October 2026

New

  • Four new creatures in the Shallows: the Reef Turtle, Old Barnacleback, the Razorfin and the Clubclaw, plus green Scute armour made from what they drop.
  • The Shallows Naturalist, and the Naturalist's Reliquary to earn.
  • The Meridian Exchange: Meridian's new central market.
  • Meridian is rebuilt with new coral towers, halls, domes, a stern tavern, a whalebone lodge and more.
  • Founding a guild: visit Registrar Quill at her desk in Meridian's square (or the Guild tab). Founding costs 500 tidemarks.
  • Guild den stations show a hologram where one can be built.
  • A new longest-kill stat, and Long Shot now counts against any creature.
  • A new jump animation: a push-off, a floaty tuck, a drift and a soft landing.

Changed

  • Party loot is now shared carving: each cut goes to whoever carves it, one body's worth between you, and chests are first come, first served. Greed and Pass rolls are gone.
  • Every carve now gives the creature's main material.
  • Named rares now hunt you on sight.
  • Bandages, poultices and tonics take a moment to put on.
  • Guild stations cost a flat ten times their normal price.
  • Sonic Shriek now shows as waveform rings.
  • Fewer lurkers in the Shallows.
  • The hurt effect is softer: a patchy red haze instead of sharp veins.
  • The Drowned Light is bigger and half-sunk in rubble.

Fixed

  • Take all now waits for the server, so nothing goes missing from your bags or Den.
  • The murk and kelp haze no longer outlines far-off rocks and buildings.
  • Small lights no longer flare against walls, and glowing creatures' lights don't blow out.
  • World map labels no longer overlap.
  • Several buildings in Meridian no longer float or sit in the rock.

Release 11

21 October 2026

New

  • Guilds: start a guild or join one by invite. Guilds have ranks (recruit, member, officer, leader): officers can invite, promote and remove members, and a leader can hand the guild over or disband it. A leader who's away for a long time hands over to the next in line.
  • The guild den: a hall beside the Dens, shared by your guild's members and seen only by them. It has a guild bank (a chest for members and an officers' vault behind its own gate) with a log of what went in and out, and guild stations (workbench, forge, tanning rack and kelp press) that officers build and upgrade.
  • Parties: invite friends into a party to see their health and a marker for each member, with their distance.
  • Party loot rolls: when your party kills something, each drop pops up for everyone to Greed or Pass (G and P keys work too), and the server rolls for whoever wants it. The results show in party chat.
  • Party and Guild chat tabs.
  • Photo mode: hide the HUD and move the camera freely to take screenshots.
  • Meridian is rebuilt: a gatehouse against the west canyon wall with the way in under a lantern arch, new landmarks, the Undercroft and the Terraces, and buildings you can go inside.

Changed

  • Crabs reach out and pinch with their claws, with a wind-up you can see coming.
  • The XP bar now sits in your status panel, shows how far along you are, and gives the exact numbers when you hover over it.
  • Your legs stop running the moment you jump or drop off a ledge.
  • Den stations have one clear label (locked, ready, missing materials or maxed), and a green arrow shows over a station you can upgrade right now.
  • Combat damage is worked out on the server, so hits count the same for everyone.
  • Iron veins no longer block the road into Meridian.

Fixed

  • Bodies can be carved again after a trip to your Den, lurkers no longer shrink, and a creature's level and quality match what it drops (hotfix 10.1, now part of the full release).
  • A creature that comes back and is killed again gets a fresh body to carve.
  • Creatures or objects that no longer exist on the server don't linger in your game any more.
  • Fair crab hits are no longer flagged as suspicious when you turn.
  • Future hotfixes can require an update on their own, so everyone stays on the same version.

Release 10

20 October 2026

New

  • Weapon bonuses by weapon type: every weapon rolls from a shared pool of bonuses plus its own family's (blades, guns and so on), and better rarities get more of them. A weapon's bonuses work while it's in your hand.
  • Skills look and sound the part: Sonic Shriek, slams and the other skills now show their reach and who they hit, with their own sounds.
  • Creatures wind up before they attack (jaws open, body coiled, a claw raised), so you can see a bite coming and dodge or block it.
  • Den stations change their look as you upgrade them, at levels 1, 3, 5, 7 and 10.
  • A red edge around the screen when you're hurt, instead of the whole screen flashing. You can turn it down or off in Settings > Gameplay, with an option to reduce flashing.
  • Main story guidance: the next main-story step is always shown, with a marker on the compass, minimap and world map, and a banner telling you where the story goes next.

Changed

  • Winded is now Exhausted: run out of O2 in a fight and you slow to a walk until you get your breath back.
  • Knock-outs: the screen goes black and creatures stop going for you while you choose where to wake. Waking clears your buffs and status effects, and the choice screen is clearer.
  • New characters start with the Harpoon Gun and a Salvager's Knife, both ready in your weapon slots.
  • Melee swings aim where you're looking, so you can hit small creatures on the floor and ones swimming above you.
  • Cutting kelp: holding E now chops at it with your Reef Cutter, a swing at a time.
  • Pickup messages for the same item merge into one, and big popups wait until you're out of a fight.
  • Chests, crates and lockers are all one size now, and the sealed ones look sealed.
  • Going in and out of your Den fades to black instead of jumping you across the world.
  • Carving has no percentage any more: hold E and the pieces come out one cut at a time, with any rare find on the last cut ("Something glints inside... a Pearl!", with the item's icon).
  • A tidier introduction: you can't swim off the ledge until you finish or skip the lessons, skipping gives you everything the lessons would, the narration keeps up as you finish steps, and the screen points out what to click when it asks you to craft or buy.
  • The glowlamp step comes later in the introduction and fits your people: a Glowlamp for humans, a Glowsight Infusion for fishmen.
  • "Not now" stops what the person you were talking to was saying.
  • The Den has been redesigned with more room, and build previews no longer show rings or decorations inside them.
  • Trade ranks explain themselves: hover a requirement like "Cooking Adept" to see that trade's ranks and how to earn them. The craft panel hides quality when it doesn't matter.

Fixed

  • Cliffs no longer turn to sand when you're more than a few metres away.
  • Divers no longer get stuck swimming in a sitting pose after a leap.
  • Creatures left frozen after a Den trip, taking no damage and healing back, are gone.
  • Fewer false movement and reach flags from the server: when you log in, during lunges, and when several traders share a name.
  • A fishman's aiming hand is fixed.
  • The species screen shows the right starting kit.
  • Decorative crates no longer look like you can loot them.
  • Building a Den station never asks for materials that only that station can make (the Forge needed Bronze Ingots).
  • Quest marks (! and ?) stay above the NPC's head instead of floating in the wrong place.

Release 9

20 October 2026

New

  • Trades: ten trades to level up as you make things, each with ranks, a mastery, bonuses at every rank, and recipes that unlock as you rank up.
  • Trade specialties: each rank lets you pick a specialty that makes your trade better in its own way.
  • The Brewery and the Loom: two new stations for your Den. Saltreach and Meridian have a Brewery anyone can use, and Spinecrest has a Loom.
  • Drinks and feasts: brew drinks at the Brewery, cook remedy dishes in the Kitchen, and share a Tide Feast or a Master's Platter.
  • Hides: tan creature hides at the Tanning Rack and make scale armour from them at the workbench.
  • Woven bags: weave a pouch, a satchel or a pack at the Loom. You now have three bag slots in your inventory.
  • Skill ranks: skills go up to rank 3, using rank materials, with pips showing each skill's rank.
  • Respecs and loadouts: change your skills at a shrine, and keep up to three loadouts to switch between.
  • Codices: two codex slots (opened at level 15 and in the Midnight depths) and four role codices: Tidemender, Bulwark, Fanatic and Warden. Shellbreaker now counts as a Bruiser.
  • Threat: creatures now pick who to attack by threat, so tanks can hold their attention and healers draw it.
  • Finishing The Drop now rewards you with a codex.
  • PvP: flag yourself for PvP at a shrine. Only two flagged players can fight. Safe zones stay safe, and dying in the Shallows costs nothing. Anywhere else, the killer gets first pick of your dropped bag.
  • A knock-out screen: when you're knocked out you choose where to wake, at any hearth you've found or in your Den, instead of being moved straight away.

Changed

  • Quality is reset once: everything in your bags and Den storage becomes plain quality, and matching items are merged into full stacks to free up space. Gear you're wearing isn't touched, and nothing is lost.
  • Tidewater Tonic is now made at the Brewery.
  • Trade XP has been rebalanced so ranks come at a steady pace.
  • PvP is clearer: a HUD chip shows when you're flagged, and you get warnings before a fight can start.
  • Swimming goes where you look: hold swim, move forward and look up or down to rise or sink.
  • The map zooms in and out on the spot under your mouse.

Fixed

  • Creatures no longer freeze at 1 health with no body after the last update.
  • Bell Ledge: the training lurker is visible again, and the introduction now puts the knife in your hand for you.
  • The ledge bench and the cooking fires in town are back.
  • The shrine panel now opens properly when you're playing online.
  • Talking to someone new stops the last person's voice, so conversations no longer talk over each other.
  • A scatter net that hits a townsperson no longer swells into a giant net.
  • If you somehow have no Reef Cutter, you're given one, so you can always cut the coral to make tools.
  • "Open the bell's locker" no longer sticks on screen if you leave the Bell Ledge mid-lesson; you get the starter kit instead.
  • The training lurker and crab at Bell Ledge look like the real creatures.

Release 8

19 October 2026

New

  • The Dyrhald Launcher: a proper app in place of the old Play Dyrhald batch file. It shows what's new and whether the servers are up, downloads updates with a progress bar, keeps itself up to date, and has a Repair button that re-checks every game file.
  • Getting it: download Dyrhald-Launcher.zip (attached on Discord) and unzip it into your game folder, or into an empty folder for a fresh install, then run Dyrhald Launcher.exe. After that it updates itself.
  • Dens have a server of their own: your Den door takes you there and back to the door you came in by, and your meal, Rested, charm cooldown and quest timers come with you.
  • Bell Ledge: a new-player introduction with its own private training creatures, walking you through your knife, cutting kelp, making a Kelp Poultice and your first fight.
  • Winded: a combat move that empties your O2 leaves you doubled over and gasping for a moment, unable to swing, shoot, dash or block until you catch your breath.
  • Quest tracking: a new quest is tracked on your HUD, compass and maps; Track and Untrack it in the journal.
  • Den stations have markers: a ring on the sand, a name tag when you're near, and a hologram where one isn't built yet.
  • Kelp Poultices can be made by hand from your bags' Crafting tab, no station needed.
  • The XP bar is always on screen, as a strip above your bars.

Changed

  • The game is now less than half the size on disk (about 1.5 GB, was 3.7 GB), and future updates download only what changed, often just a few MB.
  • This update is a full download one more time (about 1.5 GB) while the game moves to the new layout.
  • The launcher now clears out old game files it no longer needs, freeing about 3 GB on your drive.
  • Up to 80 players per world server (was 8).
  • Stamina is gone: O2 is the one pool for breathing, swimming and fighting.
  • Swings and blocks cost more O2 when you chain them (one and a half times, then more than double), back to normal after a short pause.
  • Low-level creatures are a bit tougher: half again as much health at level 1, fading to normal by level 20.
  • Crafting is simpler: pick Best quality or Use up low quality, and the game picks the stacks for you, from your bags and your Den storage.
  • Chat has a backing behind it so it's easier to read.
  • The Drowned Light is bigger and set deeper into the seabed, with a proper inside.
  • The big tube sponges are solid now; you no longer swim through them.
  • The Rested message has a backing so it's easier to read.

Fixed

  • The What's new screen no longer opens over the new-player introduction.
  • Eels' tails no longer kink and bend the wrong way.
  • The Crag Puffer in the Rimlands puffs up and stings like a puffer instead of bolting like a small fish.
  • Den doors take you to your own home door, not a stray copy.

Release 7

19 October 2026

New

  • Carving: creatures now drop their loot when you carve them, and everyone who helped gets their own share.
  • Hearths: you wake at the nearest hearth you've found when you're knocked out.
  • Ammo pouches are kept by the server, and carving creatures can give you scrap.
  • An out-of-ammo hint tells you when a gun is empty and what it needs.
  • Poison and bleeding build up from bites, fangs and spines; cure them with the new Kelp Poultice or a Patch Bandage, which no longer needs scrap.
  • Crafting: station recipe lists, a search box, categories, and an ingredient picker that shows the quality you'll get.
  • The Drowned Light: a sunken lighthouse with quest finds inside.
  • Dropped items and anything that doesn't fit in your bags land as pouches in the world.
  • Den upgrades take what they need straight from your storage chest.
  • Settings: pick your screen resolution (with Keep or Revert) and customise your crosshair.
  • A What's new screen, and new music.
  • Bags-full notices: the server tells you when something didn't fit, and it lands as a pouch beside you.
  • Giant and bull kelp have new veined leaves.
  • Crafting stations play a working animation when you use them.
  • Missing skill, adaptation and item icons are added.
  • Contracts are back on the contract board, daily and weekly.

Changed

  • Named rare creatures are much tougher (three times the health), and at most two are out at a time.
  • Chat is easier to read; only lines starting with / are emotes.
  • Clearer XP and loot text, and backings behind on-screen prompts.
  • Updates are now required: when a new release is out, the game asks you to restart so the launcher can update you.
  • If you lose your connection, the game stops and shows a Disconnected screen with the reason, Reconnect and Quit.
  • Older characters are moved over to the new gear kit automatically.
  • At the edge of the map, a current pushes you gently back.
  • Combat controls: with a gun out, right mouse aims and zooms; with a melee weapon, right mouse blocks; heavy swings hold at the full backswing.
  • The camera can pull back at most 4 m.
  • Full-piece wetsuits are no longer craftable.
  • Red "!" alert markers only show for creatures within 40 m (they used to show from across the map, even in your Den).
  • Quality merging: clicking never merges stacks of different quality; dragging asks first (or merges quietly if you ticked "don't show").
  • Station screens: Use and Upgrade are separate tabs, tooltips draw on top, and repairs are done at the bench only.
  • Named rares come and go on staggered timers.
  • The server limits damage: hits faster or harder than your weapon allows are cut down.

Fixed

  • Invisible creatures that could still hit you.
  • Being pushed back or kicked for brushing against walls.
  • The sword plunge exploit.
  • The Shrine of Echoes step, and copper ore's tool.
  • Nim in Kelpwatch and townsfolk in Meridian no longer walk or stand inside crates and props.
  • Chat messages now send properly; plain text was being mistaken for an unknown emote.
  • Feet no longer sink into the sand after swims, dives and dashes.
  • Nessa's crab pot and the Hollow Spine stone can be reached again.

Release 6

18 October 2026

New

  • Dyrhald is now online-only: your character lives on the server, so it's the same wherever you log in.
  • New guns, and ammo pouches to carry more shots.
  • Unique items: one-of-a-kind finds with their own looks and bonuses.
  • Quality and rarity on what you gather and craft: better materials make better gear.
  • The human kit: humans breathe and dive with gear that grows with them.
  • Character looks: choose how your diver looks when you make them.
  • Quick sell from your Den: right-click anything in your bags or storage chest and sell it at a trader's price.
  • The auction: right-click an item and list it for other players, then collect your earnings in the inventory's Auction tab.
  • The Diver's Handbook: a page for each thing you learn, unlocked as you meet it.
  • Stats, achievements and leaderboards, in the inventory screen.
  • Hearth respawn: set your hearth at a town's cooking fire, and wake there if you're knocked out.
  • Press F8 to send us a bug report straight from the game.

Changed

  • Melee is reworked: weapons swing, block and land hits more clearly.
  • Skills are reworked, with clearer choices in each tree.
  • The starting area is calmer, so you can find your feet before the fighting starts.
  • A new look for the HUD and every menu, with a compass and one tab bar for the map, quests, inventory, skills, the Bestiary and the Handbook.
  • "Press E" prompts sit on a backing, so you can read them on bright sand and in the dark.
  • Repair, upgrade and salvage show up only at a workbench, as their own tab.

Fixed

  • Townsfolk and quest givers behave and stand where they should.
